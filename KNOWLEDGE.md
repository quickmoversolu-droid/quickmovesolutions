# PART 1 — PROJECT VISION AND DEVELOPMENT INSTRUCTIONS

## 1.1 The Mission

We are building a premium digital platform for QuickMove Solutions.

This is not simply a website redesign.

The long-term objective is to build a complete digital operating system that helps QuickMove Solutions attract customers, manage inquiries, prepare quotations, schedule jobs, manage employees, track operations, and market its services.

The platform must combine five major systems:

1. **Premium Public Website:** A beautiful, modern, conversion-focused website.
2. **Customer Acquisition System:** Advanced moving, delivery, and junk removal quote experiences.
3. **Business Management System:** A real CRM and operational dashboard.
4. **AI Marketing Manager:** An intelligent marketing department that creates, schedules, and publishes social media content.
5. **Booking and Quotation System:** A connected system for preparing estimates, managing quotations, scheduling jobs, and tracking completion.

All these systems must work together through a shared backend and database.

The final result should feel like a premium software product built by an experienced digital agency.
